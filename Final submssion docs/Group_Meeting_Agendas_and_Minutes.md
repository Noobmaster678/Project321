## How to use this document

For each meeting, copy the template at the bottom and fill in:

1. **Date, time, location** (in-person, Teams, etc.)
2. **Attendees** — all group members present; note any apologies
3. **Agenda** — topics planned before the meeting
4. **Minutes** — what was discussed, decided, and assigned
5. **Action items** — who will do what by when

Keep minutes factual: decisions made, scope changes, supervisor feedback received, and work allocated.
